Cornerstone guide · CPG wholesale

The PO-to-cash workflow is one story told across too many systems.

For a CPG brand, purchase-order-to-cash begins when a retailer or distributor issues the PO and ends only when payment, deductions, evidence, and exceptions are reconciled. The work spans operations and finance, but the purchase order is the shared thread.

Direct answer

The workflow is healthy when every handoff preserves context, evidence, ownership, and the next action.

AuthorJordan Harper, CEO, EasyOps

Last reviewedAugust 26, 2026

Evidence statusApproved product context and qualitative demo research

Update cadenceReview quarterly and after material workflow changes

Current distributor documents and portal instructions govern.

Seven phases

Follow one retailer-issued PO all the way through.

See the living workspace →
01

Order received

Retailer-issued PO enters through EDI, email, or a portal.

Record: PO, terms, dates, item detail

02

Order confirmed

The brand acknowledges, resolves exceptions, and aligns owners.

Record: Acknowledgement, changes, decisions

03

Fulfillment prepared

Warehouse, 3PL, carrier, and team coordinate routing and documentation.

Record: Routing, appointment, packing, ASN

04

Shipment executed

The order moves and proof accumulates as events occur.

Record: BOL, scans, POD, photos, messages

05

Invoice and remittance

Invoice and payment detail arrive in different systems and formats.

Record: Invoice, remittance, bank transaction

06

Deduction investigated

Any variance is matched to the PO, cause, rule context, and evidence.

Record: Deduction line, evidence, decision

07

Closeout and learning

The outcome is recorded and recurring causes feed prevention.

Record: Resolution, owner, root cause, next control

Control points

The best deduction workflow begins before a deduction exists.

Recovery matters, but upstream context determines how quickly a team can understand what happened and whether the issue can be prevented next time.

Before shipment

Confirm terms, timing, routing, ownership, and required documents before the truck moves.

During fulfillment

Capture the evidence trail while it is easy to obtain, not months later when a deduction appears.

At remittance

Match payment and deduction details back to the same operational record.

After resolution

Turn recurring failure patterns into upstream checks and clearer accountability.

Where teams lose context

System handoffs

EDI transports transactions, the WMS manages warehouse execution, email holds exceptions, and portals hold retailer-specific detail.

Role handoffs

Sales, operations, finance, the broker, warehouse, 3PL, and carrier each see only part of the order.

Time handoffs

A deduction can appear after the people and evidence closest to fulfillment have moved on to newer work.
