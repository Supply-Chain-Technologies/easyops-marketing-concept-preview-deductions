Content architecture

Consolidated Essays into a Blog and made Guides exclusively interactive self-assessment tools.

A dated, question-shaped blog layer is what ranking competitors use and what answer engines cite; two clearly-jobbed sections beat three overlapping ones. Written reference guides moved under /blog; the architecture map, decision guide, and readiness checklist remain the interactive Guides. Production port requires 301 redirects from /essays/* and the five former /resources/* guide paths.

Cornerstone guides

These own two distinct, high-value questions: how to manage a KeHE deduction end to end, and how a retailer-issued PO moves from receipt through financial closeout. Focused K-Solve and compliance pages remain supporting references rather than competing articles.
