The audit

From deduction report to money back

Your deduction report tells you what was taken. It doesn't tell you why, whether it was valid, or how to get it back. The audit does, automatically.

  • Classifies every deduction line: contractual passthrough or disputable
  • Finds the recoverable ones and estimates the dollars
  • Matches each deduction to the PO and the rule version it cites
  • Prioritizes what to dispute first, with the evidence to attach

Sample verdict

Fill Rate 3% fee on PO-88214: dispute it

BOL and signed POD show 100% of ordered cases shipped. The shortage was a DC miscount, and your UDR response is on file inside the 48h window.

Frequently asked

Questions about the audit.

How long does the audit take?+

About 5 minutes. Upload your file, the AI classifies every line, and you get results immediately.

What file format do I need?+

Excel (.xlsx, .xls) or CSV. The K-Solve export from KeHE works directly. Other distributor exports are supported.

Is my data safe?+

Yes. SOC 2 compliant. Your data is processed and classified but never shared, stored beyond the session, or used to train models.

What happens after the audit?+

You'll see which deductions are contractual, which are disputable, and their estimated value. From there you can dispute them manually, or connect to EasyOps for continuous automation.

Is it really free?+

Yes. No sign-up, no credit card, no trial that expires. The audit is a standalone tool.

Which distributors does the audit support?+

KeHE is the deepest today with validated rules for 48 of 111 prefix codes at 100% accuracy. UNFI and other retailer deductions are classified using general rules. Coverage is expanding.
