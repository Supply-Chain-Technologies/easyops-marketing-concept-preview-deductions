Methodology

How the calculator works.

Deduction rate: 5-15%

CPG brands selling through KeHE, UNFI, and major retailers typically lose 5-15% of gross wholesale revenue to deductions. This range is documented across industry sources and confirmed in EasyOps discovery calls with 30+ CPG brands.

88% contractual / 12% disputable

In a verified audit of $151,671 in KeHE deductions, 88% ($140,914) was contractual passthrough — charges following agreed terms. 12% ($10,757) was disputable. This split varies by brand, category, and compliance maturity.

55% recovery rate

A conservative estimate for the disputable portion that can be successfully recovered. Well-documented disputes with complete evidence packets (BOL, POD, ASN, rule citation) filed within the dispute window achieve higher rates. Incomplete or late filings achieve lower.

Labor at $50/hr

Fully loaded cost for an operations or finance team member reconciling remittances, investigating deductions, and filing disputes. Actual costs range from $35/hr (junior) to $75/hr (senior), plus the opportunity cost of time not spent on other work.

This calculator provides estimates based on industry benchmarks, not your actual deduction data. Actual results depend on your specific distributor mix, product categories, compliance track record, evidence availability, and dispute timeliness. For your real numbers, upload your deduction data to the free audit tool.

Frequently asked

How accurate is this calculator?+

This calculator uses industry benchmarks, not your actual deduction data. The 5-15% deduction rate range is observed across CPG brands selling through KeHE and UNFI. The 88%/12% contractual-vs-disputable split comes from a verified EasyOps audit. The 55% recovery rate is conservative — actual recovery depends on evidence quality, filing speed, and deduction type. For your real numbers, run the free audit with your actual data.

Where do the benchmarks come from?+

The deduction rate (5-15% of gross wholesale revenue) is a range documented across CPG industry sources and confirmed by EasyOps demo-call data. The 88% contractual / 12% disputable split comes from a verified audit of $151,671 in KeHE deductions. The 55% recovery rate on disputable deductions is a conservative estimate — well-documented disputes with complete evidence packets can achieve higher rates.

What if my deduction rate is higher or lower than 10%?+

Use the slider to adjust. Brands new to wholesale distribution or with compliance issues often see 12-15%. Well-established brands with tight operations may see 5-7%. The deduction rate varies by category, distributor, and compliance maturity. If you don't know your rate, 10% is a reasonable starting estimate for a scaling CPG brand.

Can I save or share my results?+

Not yet. The calculator runs in your browser and doesn't store any data. For a permanent record of your actual deduction exposure, run the free audit — it produces a downloadable report with your real numbers.
