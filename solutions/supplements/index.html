Supplements & Vitamins

High-value units deserve deduction recovery that keeps up.

Vitamin and supplement SKUs are expensive, short-dated, and compliance-heavy. A single short-date or labeling deduction erases the margin on dozens of units. EasyOps makes every VMS purchase order a workspace that catches those charges early.

Get a demoSee deduction recovery

How EasyOps helps

Guard margin on expensive SKUs

When a unit carries real dollars, a shorted case or a short-date markdown hurts more. EasyOps quantifies exposure per PO so you triage the costly ones first.

Stay ahead of labeling and compliance charges

Supplement labels carry DSHEA and structure-function requirements; a spec mismatch becomes a compliance deduction. We trace each charge to the rule so you fix the root cause.

Manage short-dated inventory penalties

Vitamins move slower than grocery, so short-code and expiration deductions are common. EasyOps flags at-risk lots against the rule that governs them.

Short-date deduction

A markdown or refusal charge applied when product is received with less than the required minimum remaining shelf life, a frequent issue for slower-moving vitamin and supplement SKUs.

Manual today

The team discovers the short-date charge on the remittance weeks later and cannot tie it back to the shipment or lot.

With EasyOps

EasyOps links the deduction to the PO and lot, shows whether the receiving window rule was actually breached, and flags it valid or disputable.

Frequently asked

How do KeHE deductions work for a supplement brand?

Supplement brands see the same service-level deductions as grocery (fill rate, OTD) plus category-specific ones: short-date markdowns on slower-moving SKUs and labeling or spec-compliance charges tied to DSHEA and item-setup requirements. Because units are high-value, each deduction carries more margin risk, so EasyOps prioritizes them by dollar exposure.

Why do supplements get short-date deductions more than grocery?

Vitamins and supplements turn more slowly than grocery, so a shipment is more likely to arrive inside the distributor’s minimum-remaining-shelf-life window and trigger a markdown or refusal. EasyOps flags at-risk lots against the specific receiving-window rule so you can route them before they age out.

Are labeling-compliance deductions disputable?

Sometimes. If the label or case pack matches the approved item setup and the distributor scanned it wrong, the charge is disputable with the item-setup record as evidence. If the label genuinely differs from spec, it is a valid charge to fix upstream. EasyOps classifies which is which.

How does EasyOps protect margin on high-value SKUs?

EasyOps ranks open deductions by dollar exposure, not just count, so a brand with expensive units disputes the charges that actually move margin first, and sees the recurring causes to fix at the source.
