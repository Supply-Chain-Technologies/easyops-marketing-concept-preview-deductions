What EasyOps does for wholesale.

🏪

KeHE and UNFI in one view

Both major natural/specialty distributors connected. POs, remittances, deductions, and compliance requirements from each are visible in the same workspace.

Real scenarios

The DTC brand that launched wholesale without fumbling it

Before

A three-person DTC team launches into KeHE with no wholesale operations experience. They don't know what an ASN is, what a UDR requires, or that a $250 no-show fee exists.

With EasyOps

The team onboards onto the platform and learns the wholesale workflow through the system. They open KeHE, UNFI, and Target in sequence and reach $1M in wholesale revenue in four months — without hiring an ops specialist.

Before

With EasyOps

Frequently asked

Questions about wholesale.

Is EasyOps only for KeHE suppliers?+

No. EasyOps works with KeHE, UNFI, and direct retail relationships (Target, Walmart, Costco, and others). KeHE is the deepest integration today because the compliance and deduction structure is the most complex, but the platform is built for wholesale operations across distributors.

How much does it cost?+

Pricing is based on wholesale revenue, not per-seat. Up to 19 seats are included. The platform starts at $199/month for brands under $1M in wholesale revenue. Brands at $50-100M in wholesale revenue pay no more than $4,000/month.

Can I use EasyOps if I don't have an ERP?+

Yes. Some customers use Shopify as their order management system with no traditional ERP. EasyOps connects to whatever your source of truth is — Shopify, QuickBooks, NetSuite, or a custom system.

What about DSD distributors?+

EasyOps supports DSD (direct-store-delivery) distributors as well as traditional brands shipping through KeHE/UNFI DCs. The workflow is customized to the DSD model where the distributor generates the EDI and manages in-store inventory.
