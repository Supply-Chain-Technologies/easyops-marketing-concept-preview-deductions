Frequently asked

Understand the product before evaluating the demo.

Is this a new ERP?+

No. EasyOps is a coordination and intelligence layer that sits alongside your ERP, WMS, EDI, and email. It connects context from those systems so teams can see the complete operational record around each PO. It does not replace any system of record.

How long does setup take?+

Most brands are live within 2 weeks. EasyOps connects to your existing systems via API — no migration, no rip-and-replace. Distributor rules (KeHE, UNFI) are pre-loaded and version-tracked.

Does it work with UNFI?+

Yes. EasyOps supports both KeHE and UNFI, and the same PO workspace handles orders from any distributor. KeHE coverage is deepest today (111 prefix codes classified, 48 at 100% accuracy). UNFI coverage is actively developing.

Who on my team uses it?+

Operations, finance, sales, leadership, brokers, warehouses, and 3PLs. Each role sees the same PO context. The workspace is shared — no status-update emails, no forwarding for context.

How does deduction recovery work?+

When a deduction lands, EasyOps matches it to the PO automatically, identifies the cause using validated classification rules, attaches the evidence (BOL, POD, ASN), and drafts the dispute. For KeHE, disputes file through K-Solve. The evidence packet is already built from data collected during fulfillment.

What distributors are supported?+

KeHE, UNFI, Costco, Walmart, Kroger, Target, Wegmans, Albertsons, and others. The Intelligent PO workspace is distributor-agnostic — the compliance rules and deduction codes are distributor-specific.
